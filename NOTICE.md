# Attribution and portfolio scope

- Demonstrations were generated in a project-specific simulator built using PyBullet/Bullet and other scientific-computing and machine-learning tools. Those tools retain their respective licenses; their source and binaries are not distributed here.
- Spacecraft concepts draw inspiration from Kerbal Space Program, published lander concepts, and Chinese lunar-exploration concepts. The displayed spacecraft are simplified research representations, not authoritative models of flight vehicles. No proprietary game mesh or texture assets are included.
- Simulated terrain was derived from processed and rescaled NASA LRO/LOLA elevation data. Credit: NASA/GSFC/LRO/LOLA. Visualizations are not full-resolution physical landing-site reconstructions.
- Videos and images show selected simulation outcomes. They are not proof of flight readiness, generalization, or comparative superiority.
- No project-wide open-source license is granted. Public visibility does not grant a license to redistribute the research materials.

This portfolio does not distribute implementation code, model weights, training configurations, raw evaluation records, or research datasets.
